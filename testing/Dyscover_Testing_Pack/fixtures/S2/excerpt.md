CompletableFuture.supplyAsync(supplier) runs a supplier on a worker thread and returns a handle immediately. thenApply transforms a result; thenAccept consumes it; thenCompose chains another asynchronous call; exceptionally recovers from failure. join() and get() block the caller until the result is ready.
